06Result — the lesion

Ect2 is the specific lesion; RhoA only looks like one

  • Ect2 0.55× P0→P7 in cycling vCM — t = −6.17, clears Bonferroni over 25 genes
  • 3rd percentile against a genome-wide median of 0.887 over 417 genes. That is what makes it specific, not just significant
  • RhoA is the near-miss. p = 0.0004, but 29th percentile — low variance, not a large effect. We don't claim it
The maturation coordinate is measured, not fitted M = z(FAO) − z(glycolysis), per cell. Within cycling vCM alone (n = 89): Ect2 r = −0.563, E2f6 r = +0.396.

10Result — the prediction

Our knockout converts at P0, and barely at P7

  • Division +0.107 at P0 vs +0.005 at P7 — a 22× difference
  • Ect2 rises at both (+0.448 / +0.258). The machinery goes up either way; only P0 converts it
  • Real epistasis — the double is not the sum of the singles (+0.18 on Ect2 at P0). Our data is a double
Pre-empt this Our observed cycling is up at P7, not P0. Not a contradiction: this is a claim about conversion to division, not entry — and entry rises at both ages here too (+0.160 / +0.082).
An entry-scored readout would call this a hit at both ages — which is the point model_results.json → ko · fig10

11What would settle it

Two experiments would falsify this; one panel change unblocks our own cells

Ect2 rescue

Ect2 overexpression ± clonidine in mouse neonatal CM. Readout: AurKB⁺ midbody count.

Clonidine alone gives zero midbodies in >10,000 cells. If Ect2 is rate-limiting, this should produce them.

Falsified if no midbodies appear — the block is elsewhere (Anln, Cep55, or non-transcriptional).

Is there a critical M*?

Same clonidine dose in vivo at P1 vs P7. Read cardiomyocyte number, mononucleation, ploidy, midbodies.

Tests the central claim directly: above some maturation, added entry cannot add cardiomyocytes.

Falsified if CM number scales with entry at every maturation level.

Widen the curated panel

Only 29 of 63 model node genes are in the 2,181-gene panel. E2f2–E2f6, Rb1, Ccng1, Pkmyt1, Rhoa all missing.

No new experiment — a rebuild of the bundle.

Unblocks scoring the whole network on our own cells, not just its E2F layer.

A1Appendix — cross-paper consistency

Check the papers against each other before modelling anything

phase fraction ≈ rate × duration Ki-67 index ≈ rate × dwell (linear) cumulative EdU = 1 − e^(−rate·window) (sublinear) ΔEdU ≈ ΔBinuc + ΔPoly + 2·ΔDiv
  • Hidden arrested pool. Imaging implies 2.20% mVenus⁺; flow saw 5.10% → apparent S/G2/M of 38.1 h. The reported 24.5 h is a right-censored lower bound
  • Mouse arm self-consistent, nothing fitted. Predicts 32.8% cumulative EdU vs observed 25.54% — 1.28×
  • Wrong sign for a rate change. Ki-67 2.12× but EdU 2.78×. EdU saturates, so this ordering is impossible for any rate effect — evidence for non-productive cycling
Six checks, two flag a real tensioncmcycle.preflight · fig6

A2Appendix — caveat

Cycling fractions in this dataset are a sorting artefact

  • P0 essentially unenriched — 32.4% observed vs 32.5% expected in vivo
  • P7 is 4.5–5.2× enriched — 45.0% vs 8.7–10.0%
  • So the raw cycling fraction rises P0→P7 in the data and falls in reality.
What this bounds Only within-group expression contrasts are valid on these cells. Phase and cycling fractions must come from Baniol's imaging. This is exactly why lane B contributes topology only.
Baniol 2021 FACS-sorted Smart-seq2results.json → sort_enrichment · fig7

A3Appendix — “isn't Ect2 circular?”

Yes, partly — and the residual bias runs against our result

  • Ect2 (G2/M) and E2f8 (S) are in the Regev/Tirosh lists behind scanpy.tl.score_genes_cell_cycle — among 21 panel genes. So cycling-vs-noncycling contrasts are partly self-fulfilling for those genes
  • But our findings live on the P0→P7 within-cycling axis, which is not circular in the same way — and the residual bias runs against the Ect2 result: selecting cells called G2M enriches for high Ect2 in both groups, compressing the difference we report
  • The clean comparators — RhoA, Racgap1, Cep55, Ccnb1, Ccna2 — are all outside the lists
  • Quantified on the KO data: E2f8 carries 0.15–0.54% of the S-set mean, changes the call for 0.9% of cells, and shifts the KO−WT cycling gap by ≤0.5 pp
Why the audit still mattered It was a false alarm — but the audit is what established that, and it is what tells us which comparators are safe to lean on.

A4Appendix — the E2F layer

The E2F family splits into three roles

  • E2f1/7/8 — cycling-restricted
  • E2f2 — near-absent unless cycling, rises with maturation (t = +2.18). The endoreplication candidate
  • E2f6 — the only member expressed in noncycling cells, reaching 0.38 in 100% of noncycling P7 vCM (t = +4.12). The exit enforcer
  • E2f7 is flat across maturation (t = −0.20) while E2f8 rises (t = +2.28) — so they must be separate model nodes
Edges tested before wiring them E2f1~E2f7 +0.49, E2f1~E2f8 +0.46 — the delayed-feedback arm, precisely the arm our KO removes. E2f1~E2f6 +0.18 n.s., so E2f6 sits on its own maturation branch. E2f8~Ccna2 −0.56 confirms E2F8 is G1/S-restricted.

A5Appendix — “the transcripts went up?”

The knockout is functionally real; the elevation is the expected signature

  • E2f7 and E2f8 mRNA are higher in KO than WT — log2FC +0.82 and +1.57 at P7, both p < 1e−10. Which looks alarming until you check the targets
  • 24 of 26 canonical E2F targets significantly up at P7 (median log2FC +0.91) against a housekeeping/sarcomeric baseline of +0.19. At P0, 20 of 26 (median +0.50) against −0.02
  • decoupleR HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS regulon activity, cardiomyocytes: KO−WT = +1.12 at P0 and +3.15 at P7
The mechanism is the feedback loop itself E2f1/7/8 are all E2F1-induced. Losing the repressors' protein function raises E2F1 activity, which transcriptionally induces all three — exactly the delayed-negative-feedback arm validated in the Baniol data (E2f1~E2f7 ρ = +0.49). So “KO not transcript-confirmed” should be restated: not transcript-null, and functionally confirmed.
Supersedes the caveat currently in the repo READMEko_export/tbl_e2f_regulon.csv · RESULTS.md §8

A6Appendix — the engine

One property of the formalism produced the same bug four times

  • Per reaction, reactants combine by AND; the (w, n, EC50) triple belongs to the reaction, not the reactant
  • Per node, every reaction combines by weighted OR — and OR can only add drive, so no route can be vetoed by another
  • An inhibitor therefore has to be an AND term inside a reaction, never a separate reaction. Get that wrong and you silently create a floor
The worst instance An OR'd Midbody route bypassed the obligatory Ect2/RhoA arm entirely — making it structurally impossible for Ect2 to be rate-limiting. The model could not express its own central claim. Making RhoA obligatory widened the arm 1.6× → 42×. A passing test suite would have hidden all four instances.
Knockdown is Ymax→0; overexpression holds Y=1cmcycle/logic.py · fig11
